# SOLVE — Consolidated Direction, November 10, 2026

*The founder's direction, recorded verbatim. It governs product scope and build order. Only the
founder changes it. Research and ideas elsewhere are not implementation authorization.*

## Mission

Build SOLVE as five independently exceptional products sharing one trustworthy foundation.

The five consumer products are:

* SOLVE Card: physical and digital identity, introduction and connection.
* MySpace: personal expression, public presence and shareable creations.
* Bench: create and accomplish real work, independently or together.
* Control: understand, authorize, revoke, recover and export.
* My AI + Avatar: one continuous, customizable intelligent companion, with optional visual or voice embodiment.

Home is the entrance. Connect is the external interoperability layer. Neither is a sixth consumer vertical.

The shared kernel models identity, things, relationships, capabilities, authority, actions, evidence and history. Preserve its verified capabilities.

## Product philosophy

Maximum relevant capability. Minimum friction. Human control.

Each product must be independently useful and capable of reaching S-tier quality in its own category.

SOLVE owns the universal design and trust standards. Each product has its own specialized experience. Each individual owns their expression and authorized state.

The physical SOLVE Card is our reference for making substantial digital capability available through an exceptionally simple real-world interaction. It is not itself the root identity, an authentication credential or a payment card.

My AI and Avatar are one product-level companion, but intelligence, memory, permissions, personality, voice and appearance must remain technically separable.

Use leading specialist products and providers where appropriate. Do not recreate their disciplines unnecessarily.

A connected capability must be honestly classified as native, connected, a handoff or unavailable.

Screenshots shared for idea validation are not automatically approved visual targets.

## Scope discipline

Do not create additional foundational consumer products.

Maintain three authoritative levels:

1. SOLVE Constitution — fixed purpose and principles.
2. Product Charters — standalone promise, S-tier benchmark, experience standards and exclusions.
3. Current Build — one measurable user outcome.

Research and ideas are not implementation authorization.

Only one primary consumer experience may be under active development at a time. Security-critical fixes are a separate bounded exception.

Do not redesign the kernel unless a verified requirement makes it necessary.

## Immediate assignment

Inspect the latest solve/from-zero branch, relevant product decisions, actual staging deployment, approved visual references and acceptance evidence.

Verify the current repository and environment state; do not assume this document describes the latest commit.

The first consumer milestone is:

> An ordinary person creates a beautiful SOV Card and personal Space, shares it, and another person can immediately use the result without installing SOLVE.

Start by running or observing the documented real-person acceptance experience with the founder and his brother wherever feasible. Do not report human acceptance from automated fixtures.

Identify the single smallest missing part of the Card-to-public-Space experience. Implement that part to excellent functional and visual quality.

Preserve existing successful collaboration, ownership, attribution and publication boundaries.

The first complete release should lead naturally toward a physical NFC/QR card, but do not spend time on manufacturing, advanced payment capability or speculative hardware before the digital exchange works.

## Security obligation

The latest MCPvault audit records that staging connector containment was applied, but legacy clients still have broader read access than intended.

Reconcile actual deployment state, contain exposure, rotate compromised credentials where applicable, and complete a safe migration to per-application scoped authority.

Do not expand external-app access until the necessary permission boundary is independently verified.

Production remains unchanged without explicit founder approval.

Keep this security work separate from the active consumer-experience milestone.

## Quality standard

For each product, prove:

Independent usefulness: Someone would choose it without the other SOLVE products.

Experience excellence: It is visually, intuitively and technically exceptional for its actual purpose.

Personal expression: It can become meaningfully personal without weakening usability or protected controls.

Network compatibility: Identity, ownership, authority, evidence, history and portability remain truthful when products connect.

Use appropriate competing specialist experiences as benchmarks. Avoid generic AI-generated visual design.

Test real browser rendering, mobile presentation, persisted state, authorization boundaries and real user outcomes.

Do not call a product finished merely because its tests pass.

## Execution

Use the existing architecture wherever it is sound.

Choose one bounded improvement. Implement it. Verify it. Review regressions. Commit and push to the appropriate development branch.

Do not replace completed work, silently expand scope or reinterpret the product constitution.

At the end, report:

* What a real person can now accomplish.
* What changed and where.
* What was independently verified.
* What is still unproven or blocked.
* The next smallest improvement.

Think without limits. Build with focus. Verify with reality.

The goal is not more code.

The goal is a genuinely exceptional experience that a person loves using, trusts, and wants to share.
