# Immersive creation in SOLVE

Authorized by the founder's request to build the immersive, interactive, responsive experience in the approved vision. Binding direction: VISION-CANON.md, APP-TECHNICAL-VISION.md and BUILD-CHARTER.md.

## Outcome

Enter an immersive studio immediately; create/edit/orbit a bounded 3D concept; change dimensions, materials, position and rotation; inspect quantity/cost assumptions; run a real rigid-body drop; maintain an editable execution checklist; save/reload/import/export actual work. SOV ID and avatar creation are the first three onboarding moments; deeper personalization is optional.

Shared studios use existing Worlds, membership, kernel history and owned-bot grants. Competing edits remain explicit branches. Concepts and self reported checklist completion are not manufacturing, engineering approval or proof of real-world execution.

## Privacy and authority

Private drafts are on this device, with explicit storage failure and export. Identity modules default to exclusion from exported cards. Social connections use the existing prompted SOV flow. Profile drafts do not claim registered identities.

The map consumes the existing authorized spatial view. No fabricated real users or default exact location sharing. Existing Arcadia sandbox records remain illustrative sandbox records. The flat renderer does not require WebGL.

AI proposals contain bounded declarative geometry, never executable code. The in-app provider receives the chosen prompt and scene only after the person presses Send. Review precedes application. External assistants read/save under their real delegated bot actor and existing World grants.

## Engineering boundary

Primitive geometry is editable concept source, exported as JSON, GLB, metre-coordinate STL or a concept top view. It is not constraint CAD or construction blueprints. Densities and prices are editable starting assumptions. The Rapier drop isolates one upright object, uses rigid collisions and states approximations; it excludes deformation, strength, joints and certification. Editing invalidates physics.

CAD, structural solvers, lot surveys, supplier quotes, permits and financing require specialized adapters and reviewed evidence. Capability statuses expose those requirements.

## Acceptance

Desktop/mobile create/edit/undo/redo/save/reload/export; correct quantities and stale plan checks; real solver frames; profile privacy; keyboard/touch and non-WebGL projection/map; kernel privacy/replay/authorship/competing heads/revocation; full build/typecheck/lint/tests; independent review and reviewable preview.
