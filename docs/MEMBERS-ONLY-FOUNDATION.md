# Members Only — public gateway + SOLVE operational foundation

Status: founding-stage launch slice, 2026-10-26. **The agency is a funding rail, not the brand identity.** This document is an operating blueprint, not a claim that venues, insurance contracts, or global benefits are already available.

## Product boundaries

- **Members Only**: a selective human community for people from different industries to work, train, socialize, travel and collaborate voluntarily.
- **SOLVE**: the underlying identity, private communities, projects, scoped AI, evidence, and permissions. Reuse the existing SOLVE account/World records. Do not introduce a second identity system.
- **Insurance agency**: potential source of capital through valid carrier/IMO commission arrangements, once licensed and appointed. Do not imply membership guarantees earnings, employment, an insurance appointment, global access or venue use.
- **Nodes**: physical places and temporary hosted work trips, only described as current benefits when agreements, capacity, insurance and operating rules are actually in place.
- **Isaac**: a possible partner for traveling remote solar teams / shared stays, pending his consent and the economics. Do not label him a partner publicly without agreement.
- **SOV** remains an internal primitive, never a membership reward or recruiting incentive without separately approved authorization and compliance review.

## Current public slice

- Public landing page at `/members`, inside the existing SOLVE Next.js app.
- Separate premium public layout that **does not change the SOLVE workspace** at `/`.
- Present-tense claims are limited to a founding circle taking shape. Global nodes and member benefits are clearly future vision.
- Community inquiry CTA points to the known public Instagram account `@membersonly.os`: immediate person-led conversion without pretending a not-yet-activated database form works.
- Explicit bridge to SOLVE workspace and existing login/profile flow; no invented membership verification, no premature claims of paid access.
- Accessible responsive layout, basic metadata, no trackers or new data collection.
- Production domain routing / Vercel rewrite remains a separate deployment decision; `MembersOnly.OS` is currently an Instagram handle, not evidence of an owned domain.

## The funnel to implement and measure

`Founder Instagram / organic content / paid ad → /members → right-fit inquiry → personal conversation → acceptance or referral → a real event → active participant → meaningful collaboration → recurring member`

Two separate application journeys when persistent intake is ready:

1. **Community path**: interests, city, work style, consent, optional referral; no required insurance recruiting.
2. **Licensed-producer path**: licensing/appointments, industry experience, work preference (agency-supported vs independent), honest compensation explanation; use appropriate ads categories and agency compliance.

Keep intake separate from signed-in SOLVE identity until a person consents to join; do not leak private applications to other members. The application form requires an approved privacy notice, anti-spam controls, secure storage with least-privilege review, data retention and deletion policy, accurate submission acknowledgement, and a working human follow-up process. Do not ship a dead or insecure form.

Do not add Meta Pixel / Conversions API before privacy and consent are designed. Track only reliable metrics; distinguish advertising click events from verified completed applications and real attendees.

### Illustrative funnel (not a forecast)

At 1,000 real landing-page visitors:
- 8% ask for an introduction → 80 inquiries.
- 50% meet initial criteria → 40 qualified inquiries.
- 50% schedule a conversation → 20 bookings.
- 70% attend → 14 conversations.
- 43% of conversations agree to attend a founding session → 6 participating people.

The fragile assumptions are conversion and attendance. Validate each with first 100 visitors, not IG likes or broad reach. Never describe these illustration rates as measured results.

## Capital gate / action → reaction

Agency funding is *after* operating expenses, producer compensation, chargeback reserve, appropriate taxes and other risk reserves. Example only:

If truly distributable profit is `$10,000/mo`, cap experimental Members Only funding at e.g. `$1,000–$2,000/mo` pending repeatable profit and liquidity. One `$3,000` party represents 30% of that monthly cash and should not become fixed spending on one excellent commission month.

Use written, separate operator agreements. Insurance, community operations and SOLVE technology should have distinct revenue streams, financial statements and member terms; lawful intercompany financing should be documented and reviewed.

## What “proof” looks like

- 12 founding people willing to meet more than once.
- 6 active participants without founder reminders.
- 3 useful introductions with participant consent.
- 1 meaningful shared project completed by two independently accountable people.
- 1 event repeatable by someone other than the founder.
- Real, bankable insurance-agency contribution before funding ongoing venue obligations.
- No false claims about membership, pay, venue availability or worldwide reciprocal access.

## Technical sequence

**Now:** `/members` public gate → IG-assisted introduction → real human outreach.

**Next:** Secure structured application + CRM + explicit privacy/consent → source attribution → founding-member invite status. Use the existing SOLVE kernel for admitted identities and events, not a parallel SOV ID.

**Then:** Member onboarding → roles/permissioned community → project World → optional self-reported skills (clearly labeled) → introductions by opt-in → outcome/evidence record.

**Later:** Node inventory, signed host agreements, booking quotas, check-in, safety rules, travel workflows, hosted events, global reciprocal accounting, AI coordination under delegated authority.

Do not create fake inventory, inaccessible “apply” buttons, speculative member rewards, or silently turn community applicants into financial-product leads.
