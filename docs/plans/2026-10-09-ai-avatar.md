# Your AI avatar: one character that grows

2026-10-09. A project of its own. The north star (how good it has to be, and why no one else is this) is `docs/plans/2026-10-10-avatar-north-star.md`. Owner: Claude (`lib/bot/`, `app/bot/`). The avatar's look and the companion are Doris's work, and the "Your AI" screens are in Codex's unmerged rebuild. Both are coordinated below.

The founder: *"We want this to be a gamified avatar experience, where people can level up their ai avatar within the app with real skills and useful behaviors. The ability to send a sub set of agents under it to do work within their app. But one consistent ai avatar, we want to pull in 3rd party ai llms and feed this one with shared memory and context build into the app in a beautiful way."*

## In one paragraph

Everyone gets one AI avatar. It is the same character whichever AI model is thinking for it, because who it is lives in solve: its personality, its memory, its skills and its record. Claude, ChatGPT and other models plug into it and feed one shared memory. It grows by doing real work. A skill rises only when the record shows the avatar did that thing well, and you approved it. As it grows, it earns a crew: small helpers it sends to do jobs in your Worlds. The crew works under its name, inside limits you set, and reports back. Its growth is shown visually: the blank mannequin fills in as it earns trust.

## Principles

1. **One identity, many brains.** The model is the engine, not the character. Switching from Claude to GPT never changes who your avatar is.
2. **Levels come from the record; trust comes from you.** Doing the work raises a skill. Only your yes gives it new permission, and you can take that back.
3. **The crew works under the avatar.** Helpers are the avatar's hands, never new characters (master spec: "do not create a second conceptual identity"). Each one gets one job, a time limit and a budget, and stops when the job closes.
4. **One shared memory, sealed and erasable.** Everything any brain learns that you keep goes into the same memory, labeled with which AI brought it. Forgetting erases it for good.
5. **Beautiful and calm.** The game is in the feeling: your character, its growth, its crew. It is never near money or voice (brand §6).

## The design

### 1. Who the avatar is: one home

The avatar is its persona, soul.md, face, memory, skills and record. Persona and memory are already on the kernel record. Slice 1 moves the soul.md and the face onto the same versioned persona record, so changes have history and every brain reads one source. Nothing on the person's side changes.

### 2. Brains: third-party AIs feeding one memory

There are three ways in, all landing in the same memory with a "via" label:

- **Connected assistants.** Claude, ChatGPT and others connect through the connector after you approve each one. They read the avatar's context and add to it as the avatar.
- **Imported history.** ChatGPT works today; Claude and Gemini exports come next. Each conversation becomes a sealed memory.
- **The avatar's own engines.** It picks a quick or a deep model per turn through OpenRouter (Sonnet 5.5 for deep, Haiku 4.5 for quick, both verified 2026-10-06). You can set a preferred engine.

**One context pack** (`lib/bot/context.ts`) gives every brain the same picture:
- persona and soul;
- the memories most relevant to the moment;
- the room you're in;
- what's waiting on you;
- its skills and permissions.

It is bounded and marked as data, never instructions, as the connector already does. Talk, the brief, crew jobs and the connector's `get_context` all use it. That's what makes the avatar consistent across models.

**Write-back.** "Keep this" on any Talk turn saves it as a memory, with the model that said it as the source.

**The Brains panel** shows what feeds the avatar: which assistants are connected (read, or read and add), what was imported, and which engines it uses.

### 3. Skills and levels, earned on the record

A skill is something real the avatar does in the app. Its level is computed from records that already exist, never from points.

| Skill | What it does | Rises with (records) |
|---|---|---|
| Remember | Keeps what you tell it | Memories kept and not forgotten; assistants you let remember |
| Brief | Tells you what changed | Briefs written and opened |
| Wayfind | Finds routes to a goal | Routes you took; steps you finished |
| Draft | Writes messages and notes | Drafts you approved unchanged |
| Build | Adds to your Worlds | Pieces you kept |
| Prove | Helps you save proof | Saves on the record |
| Connect | Reaches people in your communities | Notes sent for you that got replies |
| Research | Finds things via its crew | Findings you kept |

- **Tiers.** Each skill has three: *Learning*, *Reliable* and *Trusted*. Progress shows as real counts ("9 drafts you approved; 3 more to Reliable"), never XP.
- **Packs add skills.** The solar desk adds "Desk triage" and the dialer adds "Follow-ups", so expansions grow the character.
- **The avatar's level** is the sum of its skills, shown as its **form**: the mannequin gains shape, then detail, then presence. Each form is a real stage: *Blank → Shaped* (persona and first memories) *→ Known* (Remember at Reliable) *→ Trusted* (your first trust offer accepted) *→ Lead* (runs a crew).
- **Trust offers.** When a skill reaches Reliable or Trusted, the avatar asks for one specific permission, e.g. "Nova has written 12 drafts you approved unchanged. Let it pre-fill outreach for you?" Yes issues a grant or lease; no is fine. Raising a level never grants authority by itself, and nothing that sends, spends or signs ever becomes automatic.

### 4. The crew: helpers sent under the avatar

- **Roles:**
  - Scout: finds and checks things;
  - Scribe: drafts;
  - Builder: adds pieces to a World;
  - Runner: follows a route's in-app steps;
  - Watcher: keeps an eye on a build, a desk or a market.
- **How a job runs:**
  1. You ask, or the avatar proposes and you say yes.
  2. The kernel makes a task in that World and spawns a specialist agent labeled "Nova · Scout". You are accountable for it, and the avatar is its owner on the record.
  3. The helper gets a lease limited to that task, that World and a few hours. It works through the same governed path as everything else, then posts its results as pieces ("via Nova · Scout").
  4. When the task closes, its lease ends.
- **Crew size grows with the avatar's form:** one helper at Known, three at Lead.
- **Cost:** each job has a token budget shown before it starts, and the person's hourly cap still applies. The crew runs as a background job on the server with the deployment's service key, so it needs the staging server key set (the pending Vercel step).

### 5. Behaviors: habits you switch on

As skills mature, the avatar offers standing habits, each run on a schedule within granted limits and recorded each time:
- a morning brief;
- tell me when my build goes red;
- triage the solar desk;
- nudge me about quiet steps.

### 6. The look

The avatar screen centres on the character, in its current form:
- its skills sit around it as a tree of real capabilities, each with its counts and next step;
- its crew orbits it while working, live from presence;
- the Brains panel shows what feeds it;
- the record is underneath.

The companion that floats through the app uses the same form. Motion is assembly, not confetti. The copy stays plain: "Nova can pre-fill drafts now. You allowed it."

## Brand check (needs the founder)

`docs/brand/BRAND.md` §6 says "No points, XP or badges. The only score is the record", and forbids "Level up!" copy. The master spec lists "an avatar game" under what solve is not. The founder's ask now is levels.

**Proposal:**
- Levels yes, earned only from the record and shown as the avatar's form with real counts.
- No points, streaks, daily rewards or loot.
- Nothing near money, trading or voice.
- Copy stays plain.

The founder decides whether the UI may say "Level" and whether this updates BRAND.md §6.

## What we won't do

- No points, streaks, leaderboards or rewards for spending time in the app.
- No helper that outlives its job or holds permission you didn't give.
- No second character per model.
- No automatic sending, spending or signing at any level.

## Checks per slice

Focused tests on every boundary the slice touches:
- identity, so the avatar is the same across models;
- permissions, so a level never grants authority and a lease ends with its task;
- provenance, so every memory and piece names the AI it came through;
- consent and erasure;
- budget.

Browser checks when the avatar screen changes. Full suite before merging.
