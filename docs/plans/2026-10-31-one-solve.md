## The idea

One app where your memory, your ventures, your AI and your people live together, and anything you describe gets built while you watch.

- **SOV Passport is the memory.** It stays its own app (sovereign-passport.vercel.app, DECISIONS.md 2026-10-10). Solve connects to it over OAuth/MCP like Claude does, so Claude, ChatGPT and Solve all start from the same facts about you.
- **Projects are the place.** Each venture (Zest Solar, the life insurance agency, Members Only, Zest AI, the animated anthology, Zuber Pen, Solve, Sovereign Passport) is a project, filled in from the passport.
- **Your AI does the work.** Morning brief, next moves as "Do it / Change it" cards, tools inside each project. Nothing acts without your tap.
- **Your people build with you.** Invite your brother, recruits and members; their AIs and yours work from the same facts.
- **It's fully adaptable.** Say "build me ___" and a working tool, tracker, game or workflow appears in the project, changeable by talking, shareable as a Pack.
- **Your AI has a character card.** Rating, class, stats, abilities, gear and shields, all from real outcomes.
- **Integrations are upgrades.** Each connected app is gear that unlocks abilities.

## Step 3: so simple a kid could use it
Keep the brand: dark navy #070c14, one cyan accent #22d3ee, Archivo, calm plain copy, restraint over spectacle.
- Home is one screen: "What do you want to get done?" with a text box and a mic, plus up to three suggested moves from the passport and what's waiting.
- Talk, don't navigate. "Start a project for X", "invite my brother", "remember Y", "what's on today" open the existing features, already filled in.
- Every AI action is a card with two buttons, "Do it" and "Change it". Undo for 10 seconds where possible; "Forget this" on memories.
- Plain words in all user-facing copy; no "tier", "scope", "grant", "kernel", "World" or "MCP".
- Never an empty screen: every empty state has one obvious next step.
- Passkey sign-in, prefilled from the passport.
- CI first-run test at 390px: sign in → ask → approve a card → done, in under 60 seconds and 6 taps.

## Step 4: the AI character card
- Extend the existing avatar skills (lib/bot/, docs/plans/2026-10-09-ai-avatar.md). One avatar per person.
- Overall rating 0–99, level and title (Rookie → Operator → Closer → Sovereign), and a class from its abilities (Closer, Recruiter, Builder, Creator, Strategist).
- Six stats, 0–99, computed only from real kernel/passport records, each tappable to the records behind it:
  - Knowledge: passport facts/projects and approved memories.
  - Execution: approved moves that completed.
  - Accuracy: approved as-is vs changed or rejected, recent-weighted.
  - Reach: connected tools, assistants and packs.
  - Trust: access granted, with zero overreach.
  - Streak: days in a row the brief was read and a move approved.
- Abilities are slots (Morning Brief, Lead Follow-up, Recruiting Outreach, Solar Pricing, Trip Planner…), each leveling with use. Gear is connected apps; shields are hard boundaries.
- XP and a recent-wins feed linked to real records. Practice is labeled and earns zero XP. Stats can go down.
- Formulas in lib/bot/rating.ts with unit tests. Subtle level-up animation that respects reduced motion; clear at 390px.

## Step 5: build games together, play them together
- A game is a kind of project. Reuse the shared rooms (live updates, attribution, undo), the studio and Arcadia; bring them out from behind flags only as they pass Step 2.5.
- "Make a [kind] game with [people]" → a playable first version from starter kits: racer, platformer, trivia, tycoon, and a sales-floor roleplay (door-knock objection practice against an AI homeowner).
- Co-building: everyone edits live, every change is attributed and undoable, and AIs act only within granted access.
- Play: one button starts a live match everyone in the room joins, on phone or desktop. State is authoritative on the server; scores can't be faked from the client.
  - AIs can join as teammates, rivals or game master.
  - Leaderboards per game and per community.
- Publishing: room-only, community or invite-only.
- Kid-safe defaults: invite-only, no stranger chat, an adult owns the room and sees all messages, no public discovery for kids' rooms.
- Feeds the card: Creator class, Game Builder ability, XP only for games real people played.
- First game: the sales-floor trainer for insurance recruits, live on phones for 2+ players, with a leaderboard.

## Step 6: fully adaptable, say it and it's built
- "Build me ___" in any project creates a working mini-app (a Machine): tool, tracker, form, dashboard, game or workflow. It's built live and prefilled from the passport and project facts.
- Machines are versioned project artifacts (reuse World documents/revisions). Change them by talking; every change is a version you can roll back.
- Each Machine runs sandboxed: a separate-origin iframe with a strict CSP and no network except a solve API bridge that exposes only what the project's grants allow.
  - Anything with real-world effect (texts, emails, posts, payments, bookings) goes through a Do it / Change it card.
- Machines store data as attributed kernel events and are shared live with the room.
- Publish as a Pack (extend lib/community/packs.ts): one tap makes it a reusable template any community can switch on. These are the "digital machines"; a marketplace and Whop come later.
- First: "build me a recruiting tracker for my insurance agency", usable by the founder and one invited person on phones, with version history.

## Step 7: integration pathways (the AI's skill tree)
- One way to connect anything: remote MCP over OAuth 2.1, or standard OAuth. Reuse the passport-link pattern: PKCE, tokens sealed on the record, rotated on refresh, destroyed on disconnect. One generic connection module.
- Data-driven catalog (lib/integrations/catalog.ts) with honest labels (live / needs keys / coming soon):

| Path | Connect | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Communicator | Gmail/Outlook, Google Calendar, Twilio | Follow-ups, booking, reminders, power-dial |
| Closer | GoHighLevel/HubSpot/Close, DocuSign | Lead tracking, deals, signatures |
| Recruiter | Instagram/Facebook, LinkedIn, Calendly | Recruit outreach, interviews, 150-agent goal |
| Marketer | Meta Ads, Instagram Graph, Canva | Ads, posts, reporting |
| Operator | Google Drive, Notion, Slack, Linear | Docs, tasks, team briefs |
| Money | Stripe, Whop, QuickBooks | Payments, memberships, cash view |
| Builder | GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, Figma | Ship apps |
| Explorer | Maps, web search, Uber/Airbnb deep links (PR #17) | Trips, routes, research |
| Creator | ElevenLabs, video tools, Higgsfield | Voices, video, anthology episodes |

- Connecting unlocks abilities on the card; abilities level only from real approved use.
- Reads are automatic. Sending, posting, booking, signing and spending always need an approval card.
- Limits per integration (spend caps, quiet hours, who it may contact) are shown as shields and enforced server-side.
- Suggested upgrade: when the record shows friction, the AI proposes the one most useful connection and says why. It never auto-connects.
- Order: Google Calendar + Gmail first (makes the brief real), then Twilio texts, then the CRM.
