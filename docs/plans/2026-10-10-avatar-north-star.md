# The avatar, imagined: more fun than a game, more useful than an assistant

2026-10-10. The north star for `docs/plans/2026-10-09-ai-avatar.md`. Second pass; the founder asked to imagine it bigger.

The founder: *"Imagine how amazing it would have to be first. To be differentiated enough and how it could be useful as a playground for people to build and communicate virtually and really."*

## The bar, in one line

**People open it when they have nothing to do, and pay for it because it got something real done.**

If it's only fun, it's a game. If it's only useful, it's an assistant. It has to be both, and nobody has built that. Muse, Copilot and Gemini are assistants that now have faces. The companion apps are characters that don't do anything. solve's avatar is a character in a world where the work is visible, shared and real.

## 1. You meet it, and it already knows you

An empty room, one seat. A figure made of light sits up and says, "Bring me what you've told your other AIs." You connect ChatGPT and Claude. Years of conversations pour in, and you watch them stream into it. It takes shape: your taste, your rhythm, your voice.

Then it says: "You've started three businesses in your head this year. Zest Solar is the one you keep coming back to. Want to build it?" Tap any sentence and it shows where it learned it.

No one else can do this, because every other AI only knows what you told *it*.

## 2. Say an idea, watch a world assemble

You say "Let's build Zest Solar." The floor extends and a building rises: your company, as a place. Your avatar's crew appears, three small figures in its colors.

- The Scout runs to the edge of the map and comes back with five real installers, each a card with its source.
- The Scribe sits at a desk writing your first homeowner proposal.
- The Builder sets your 3D roof down on the table.

You watch real work happen as motion. It's like watching a city game build itself, except every brick is real and has a receipt. Nothing leaves the world without your yes.

## 3. It gets better, and you can see it

- **Skills, not points.** Every skill is something it has done well that you approved. Touch any skill to see the proof behind it.
- **The figure evolves.** It goes from light, to form, to detail. It gains gear for what it's proven: a lamp for writing, a lens for research. Earned, never bought.
- **The real level-up is your trust.** "I've written twelve replies you sent without changing a word. Want me to handle these from now on?" Saying yes is the moment it grows.
- **Skills spread.** Your avatar's proven Zest Solar proposal can be taught to Maria's avatar, with your OK, and it shows who taught it. People's best methods travel from avatar to avatar.

## 4. The world is full of other people's avatars

- **A living map of everyone you build with.** Their avatars are there even when they're asleep.
- **Walk up to anyone's avatar and ask.** "Can Maria take the Tampa permits this week?" It answers from what she shares and leaves her a note. Time zones stop mattering, because your avatar holds the room while you sleep.
- **Avatars negotiate; people decide.** Two avatars draft a deal, and it waits on the table until both people say yes.
- **Parties.** You, two friends and your three avatars take on a weekend build: launch a site and take the first booking by Sunday. The progress you watch is real milestones. When it's done, the save point is real: the site is live.

## 5. It steps into real life

- **It's with you.** Today's three things on your phone, a briefing in your earbuds on the drive, and on glasses when they're ready.
- **Plans become events.** It books the meeting, sends the proposal (with your yes), orders the materials, prints the part.
- **People meet.** The avatars organize community meetups; the people show up.

## 6. Every project has a replay

Scrub back through any project and watch it get built: who did what, avatar and person, step by step, with proof. Share the replay as your portfolio. Your track record becomes something you can show, not something you claim.

## 7. Any brain, one character

Brains are cores you slot in: Claude for writing, GPT for code, Muse for voice, whatever is best next year. Swap the core and keep the character. Its memory lives in solve, not in any one company's AI, so it's yours.

## Why it's untouchable

- **Others give you an assistant with a face. solve gives you a character in a world.** The work is visible, it's shared with the people you build with, and it's real.
- **The moat is the record.** Everything the avatar knows and has done belongs to you, works with any AI, and can be proven to anyone.

## Six tests it has to pass

1. **Day-one magic.** In its first session it says at least three true things about you, learned from the AIs you already use, each with its source.
2. **Same character on any brain.** Swap the model mid-conversation and the voice, memory and values hold.
3. **Visible work.** Every crew job shows live, can be stopped, and leaves a receipt with its cost.
4. **Growth you can't fake.** Every skill traces to records anyone can check. There are no points to farm.
5. **Better together.** Two people with their avatars finish a real task faster than either alone, and the replay shows who did what.
6. **The most beautiful thing on the screen.** Calm, alive and unmistakably yours. Motion is assembly, never confetti, and nothing near money is ever a game.

## First steps toward it

1. **Day-one magic.** Connect ChatGPT or Claude, and the avatar says what it already knows about you, with sources. It reuses the import and the connector that exist.
2. **Watch it work.** One Scout job, visible in the room from start to receipt.
3. **Ask my avatar.** A front door on your page; homeowners ask about solar before you're on the phone.
4. **Skills you can touch, and a figure that evolves.**

The founder still decides whether the screen says "Level", because BRAND.md §6 says no points or XP.
