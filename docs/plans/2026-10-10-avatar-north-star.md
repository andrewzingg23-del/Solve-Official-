# The avatar, imagined first: how good it has to be

2026-10-10. The north star for `docs/plans/2026-10-09-ai-avatar.md`.

The founder: *"Imagine how amazing it would have to be first. To be differentiated enough and how it could be useful as a playground for people to build and communicate virtually and really."*

## The bar

Everyone will have an AI with a face by next year. Meta showed Muse with a live video avatar and background work at Connect 2026. Microsoft's Copilot has Mico, memory and 32-person group chats. Google's Gemini Spark runs around the clock. Companion apps such as Replika and Character.AI have tens of millions of users. So an avatar that talks, remembers and works in the background is the floor, not the product.

solve's avatar has to be the one thing none of them can be:

**Yours across every AI, real in what it does, and better with other people.**

## A day with it

**Minute one.** You open solve. A blank figure stands in an empty room. It asks one question: "Which AIs do you already use?" You connect ChatGPT and Claude. It reads your history, with your yes, and fills in while you watch: your look, then your voice, then what you've been working on. "You've been trying to get Zest Solar off the ground since July. Here are three things you already decided, and one you keep putting off." Each line links to where it learned it. It knows you on day one, because it learned from the AIs you've used for a year.

**The morning.** It briefs you. Overnight its crew did three jobs:
- a Scout found five installers with open capacity, each with its source;
- a Scribe drafted replies to two homeowners on the solar desk;
- a Watcher saw your build go red at 2am and pinned the failing test.

Every job has a receipt and a cost. Nothing went out without you. You approve two drafts with a tap, and they're sent from the desk.

**The room.** You walk into the Zest Solar community room. Maria is there, and so is her avatar. Your two avatars have already compared notes, within what each of you allowed: hers knows the Tampa permits, yours knows the roof designs. Together they propose who does what this week. You both say yes, and it's on the record. A homeowner who isn't a member asks your avatar a question from your public page. It answers only from what you've marked shareable, and books a real design call on your calendar.

**The moment it grows.** Your avatar's drafts have been approved unchanged twelve times. It changes in front of you: sharper and more defined, its own. It asks: "Let me pre-fill outreach from now on?" You say yes. That's a level. It means something because anyone can see what it rests on: twelve approved drafts, on the record.

**The playground.** On Saturday you and two friends open a room to design a tiny house. Your avatars split the work: one prices materials, one models the frame in the studio, one checks the county rules. You argue about the porch in voice, in the room. On Sunday the materials list goes to a real supplier. The world you played in made something real.

## Why no one else is this

| They are | We are |
|---|---|
| **A character locked to one company's AI.** Muse, Copilot and Gemini each keep their own memory, and none can read the others'. | **One character, any brain.** Claude, ChatGPT, Muse or whatever is best next year feeds one memory you own. Changing models never changes who your avatar is. |
| **A face on a chatbot.** The avatar is a skin over the conversation. | **A face on a track record.** Its form is its record: skills earned from work you approved. Others can check it. |
| **A black box doing errands.** Background agents act broadly and report back. | **A crew you can watch.** Each helper has one job, one World, a time limit and a budget. It shows live, leaves a receipt and stops when the job closes. |
| **A group chat, or a 1:1 companion.** | **A shared world.** People and their avatars build in the same room. Avatars coordinate only within what each person allowed, and every agreement lands on the record. |
| **Engagement as the goal.** Streaks, dependency and endless chat. | **Outcomes as the goal.** A designed roof, a printed part, a booked meeting. The game is in the feeling, never in the money. |

## The playground: virtual and real

- **Build virtually.** Rooms are where people and avatars make things together: proposals, 3D designs in the studio, plans, code. Each avatar brings its skills, and packs add more.
- **Communicate virtually.** Avatars meet before people need to. "Ask my avatar" lets anyone ask someone's avatar a question from their public page; it answers only from what its person shared. Avatar-to-avatar coordination happens inside a community, within consent, and nothing binding happens without the people.
- **Real outcomes.** Anything that sends, spends, signs or meets goes through its person's yes and lands on the record as a save point. Real meetings, real deliveries and real receipts are the reason to play.
- **Real presence later.** The same avatar on your phone, in voice, and on glasses when the devices are ready. One character wherever you are.

## Six tests it has to pass

If any of these is "no", it isn't differentiated enough to ship as the headline.

1. **Day-one magic.** In its first session, the avatar says at least three true things about you that it learned from the AIs you already use, each with its source.
2. **Same character on any brain.** Switch the model mid-conversation and the voice, memory and values hold. This can be tested on every engine.
3. **Real work while you're away, all visible.** Every crew job shows live, can be stopped, and leaves a receipt with its cost.
4. **Growth you can't fake.** Every level traces to records others can verify. There are no points to farm.
5. **Better together.** Two people with their avatars in one room finish a real task faster than either alone, and the record shows who did what.
6. **The most beautiful thing on the screen.** It's calm, alive and unmistakably yours. Motion is assembly, never confetti.

## What this changes in the plan

Two slices move up and one is added to `docs/plans/2026-10-09-ai-avatar.md`:

- **Slice 1 becomes "one brain and day-one magic."** Talk reads the shared memory. Connecting ChatGPT or Claude immediately produces "what I already know about you", with sources. That's the first-minute moment, and it reuses the import and connector that exist.
- **New: "Ask my avatar".** A public front door on a person's or community's page. The avatar answers from what its person marked shareable and can open a real request, such as a solar design call or a join request. It reuses the solar desk and the community ask flow. Founder-first use: homeowners ask Andrew's avatar about solar before he's on the phone.
- **Avatars in rooms.** Live presence already shows who's in a World. Next it shows their avatars, and in a community, avatar-to-avatar coordination happens within each person's consent.

What doesn't change: one identity, levels from the record, trust only from the person's yes, and nothing near money or voice gamified (pending the founder's call on the word "Level").
