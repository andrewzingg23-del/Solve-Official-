# solve — the brand

Adopted by the founder on 2026-10-04. This is the canonical brand. The reasoning and research behind it (vision quotes, consumer research, lessons from earlier revolutions, the ad's shot list and production prompts) is in `docs/brand/2026-10-04-the-construct.md`. Brand changes go through the founder.

## The weight of the ambition

The vision is not an app. It is *"a living operating system for real life"* and, at its end, *"a programmable interface for reality."* The brand has to carry that from day one without promising it before it is true.

So the brand is built like the brands that lasted:
- **One name** that grows with the person.
- **A few iconic assets**, repeated for decades.
- **A promise** the product can prove every single time.

It is never built on a trend, a feature or a technology. AI is the electricity, not the brand.

## Core

- **Purpose:** the world should build around the person, not the other way around.
- **Promise:** stay in your seat. The world builds around you.
- **Proof:** every result is real, and on the record.

**Positioning.** For anyone with something they want to make real, solve is the world that assembles around them. It brings the people, tools and steps it takes, works with the AI they already use, and keeps an honest record of everything that happens. Other tools give you answers. solve gives you outcomes you can prove.

## Architecture

One master brand. Everything else is a descriptor, never a separate app or a rival name.

- **solve**: the brand and the product. People use it, say it and see it.
- **Sovereign**: the engine and the philosophy underneath (the kernel, the record, authority that stays with the person). Used quietly ("built on Sovereign") or internally, never as a consumer name.
- **SOV**: identity and the future economy: SOV ID, and later SOV (*reasons to use before reasons to trade*).
- **Descriptors**, plain nouns and lowercase in running text:
  - your **World**: each thing you're making;
  - **Rooms**: purpose-built spaces;
  - **Packs**: sector mechanics;
  - your **bot**: named by you, for example Nova;
  - **the record**: what happened, proven.

Name rules:
- **The wordmark is lowercase `solve`, always** (Build Book §1). In running copy, write `solve` too.
- **Never stack names** ("Sovereign solve SOV AI").
- **Never call it an AI app.**

## The ascent

The brand stays the same while the product grows into the ambition. Each step reuses the same assets, so the brand compounds and never resets.

1. **The seat:** one person, one World, one real thing made and proven. *(Now.)*
2. **The room:** Worlds with a purpose, where a person's bot and tools work together.
3. **The network:** worlds connect, and people and their bots build together across them.
4. **The economy:** communities with members, rules, treasuries and reputation (Network States) settle value on SOV.
5. **The interface for reality:** the vision's end state.

Rule: market only the step that is real. Show the next step as an ambition, never as a feature.

## Identity

- **The mark.** A point above a horizon: the person, standing on the floor of their world. It sits in `public/brand/solve-mark.svg` as a working mark until the founder commissions the final one. No crown, shield, coin or chair.
- **Color.** The interface keeps the Build Book's palette:
  - abyssal blue-black `#04070d`–`#0a1220`;
  - ice white `#f2f7fb`;
  - one cyan accent `#22d3ee`;
  - emerald `#00e5a0` for positive, red `#ff4d5e` for destructive.

  **No gold, ever.** Imagery uses real light and the construct's clean floor.
- **Type.** Archivo; engraved uppercase labels with generous tracking; tabular, stable numbers.
- **Motion: assembly.** Things settle into place with calm easing, the way a world assembles around a seat. No glitch, no particle swarms, no spinning HUD.
- **Sound.** One soft "set" tone when something lands on the record. Never notification noise.
- **Imagery.**
  - Real people, a locked frame and real places.
  - Generated imagery only where the world changes, grounded and disclosed.
  - Every story ends on proof.
- **Interface.** Calm. The World and the person first, few panels, no chrome for its own sake.

## Iconic assets, built once and repeated for years

1. **The construct ad:** you stay, the world builds. See the shot list in the construct doc.
2. **The assembly transition:** how every world arrives, in the ad, on the site and in the product.
3. **The mark:** a point above a horizon.
4. **The record seal:** "On the record". It appears only beside real, verifiable results; it is never decoration.
5. **"Ask for a way.":** the prompt that opens everything.

## Never

- Glitch effects, particle swarms, HUD dashboards on brand surfaces.
- "Agents", "superintelligence", "revolutionary", "AI-powered" as a headline.
- Income promises or projections without evidence (Build Book §10).
- Leading with the token.
- Simulated activity shown as real (Arcadia stays labeled as a sandbox).
- Copied Matrix imagery, including the red chair. The seat is the person's own.
- Sub-brands that compete with the master brand.
