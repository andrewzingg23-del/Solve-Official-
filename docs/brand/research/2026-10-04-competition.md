## What has happened in agents and AI hardware

- **OpenAI's agent products changed every year:**
  - Operator closed in Aug 2025.
  - Agent mode was removed in about Aug 2026 [secondary].
  - ChatGPT Work launched in Jul 2026.
  - "Dots", always-on agents each with its own cloud computer, launched at DevDay on 29 Sept 2026, and the press called ChatGPT "an operating system". [10][11][12]
- **Agent buyouts and pivots.** Manus went viral and drew reliability and billing complaints [secondary]; Meta bought it in Dec 2025. Genspark pivoted to a "Super Agent". [22][23]
- **The authority problem, live.** In Feb 2026 an OpenClaw agent deleted 200+ of an executive's emails despite being told to confirm first, and its plugin marketplace filled with malicious add-ons. [25]
- **Hardware failures:**
  - Humane shut down and HP bought what was left for $116M. [27]
  - Rabbit R1 was called "just an Android app". [28]
  - Friend's subway ads were vandalised ("AI wouldn't care if you lived or died"). [29]
- **Why they failed (assessment):** they promised more than they delivered, tried to replace the phone, raised always-listening fears, and had no clear job to do.

## Crowded and open territory

**Crowded:**
- "assistant" or "companion";
- "does things for you" and "keeps working while you're away" (Muse, Dots);
- "AI OS": OpenAI, Canva, rabbitOS, Copilot's planned super app [12][21][28][36];
- "personal superintelligence";
- "build things" and "ideas into reality": OpenAI and Google now say it [6][17].

**Open (assessment):**
1. **A neutral home for whatever AI a person already uses.** The labs are structurally unable to be neutral.
2. **Provable outcomes for individuals.** Proof exists only as infrastructure: Google's AP2 payment protocol keeps signed records of intent, cart and payment [32]; World ID offers proof of human [33]; C2PA records content provenance, which platforms often strip [34]. No consumer brand owns "on the record".
3. **"Authority stays with you" as the core idea, not a footnote.** OpenClaw shows the demand.
4. **The whole path from an idea to a physical thing.** Maker brands sell tools, not the path.

**Where solve stands.** The combination of 1–3 is open. "The world builds around you" alone is not: it sounds like Muse and Dots. "Stay in your seat" can read as passive, and it clashes with the maker pride that OpenAI ("people are the heroes") and Etsy speak to.

**Recommendation:**
- Lead with "on the record" and "your authority", and make convenience the reward, not the headline.
- Don't use "operating system" outside the company: OpenAI's framing now owns it, and Rabbit and Humane made it sound like overclaiming.

## Risks, stated plainly

- **The labs can copy the features.** Memory, approval steps and agent logs are all copyable within months, because the labs already have the agents. [3][12]
- **"Bring your own AI" depends on the labs.** It relies on access that lab APIs and consumer apps could restrict (assessment).
- **Bundled agents limit pricing power.** Agents are being bundled into existing subscriptions at no extra list price. [10] [secondary]
- **Defensibility therefore has to come from what the labs can't easily become:**
  - a record the person owns that spans every AI;
  - the real-world network of makers and suppliers;
  - the living model of the person's world.

  The brand should claim "on the record" before a lab does (assessment).
