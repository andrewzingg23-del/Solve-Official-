# Naming research: solve, Sovereign and SOV

Date: 2026-10-04. Part of the brand v2 research (`docs/brand/BRAND.md`).

**How this was sourced.** A research agent searched the web. The network proxy blocked direct page fetches, including USPTO mirrors, WHOIS and app stores, so the claims below come from search-engine extracts of the cited URLs, not from pages read in full. The domain checks in the table were run directly against Vercel's registrar on 2026-10-04. **None of this is legal clearance.** Get a full clearance search from trademark counsel before filing or buying paid media.

## Who already uses "solve"

- **MIT Solve:** an MIT initiative for social-impact technology. It reports 100+ challenges, 26,800+ applications from 185 countries and $80M+ mobilised. It runs an AI for Humanity Prize (2027 round closes 2 Nov 2026). Its mission, "solve world challenges with tech and AI", sits next to ours. [1][2]
- **SolveAI (London):** raised $50M in Feb 2026 (a GV-led Series A plus an Accel-led pre-seed). Employees describe what they want in plain language and get enterprise apps. It appears to run solve.ai. **This is the closest overlap in what the product does.** [3][4]
- **Solve Intelligence:** AI for patent work; $40M Series B (Dec 2025); 400+ IP teams. [5]
- **Others:** Solve Data (e-commerce data) [6]; Solve CRM by Norada [7]; many "Solve" AI homework apps; Solvely (5M+ students). [8][9]

## Sovereign and SOV

- **Extremism.** The FBI and DHS treat sovereign-citizen extremists as a domestic-terrorism threat, and the SPLC tracks the movement. [22][23][24]
- **"Sovereign AI" already means national AI.** NVIDIA uses the term for a nation's own AI capability, and the UK launched a £500M Sovereign AI Unit in April 2026. [25][26][27]
- **Crypto.** "Self-sovereign identity" is an established decentralized-identity term, and Sovereign Labs builds a rollup SDK. [28][29][30][31]
- **SOV is already a token.** Sovryn's governance token is SOV, and Coinbase has a price page for it. This collides directly with our token layer. [32][33]
- **Trademarks.** SOVEREIGN (85459940) and SOVEREIGN SYSTEMS are filed for software, and there is a **SOVEREIGN ENGINE** application (99159728). [34][35][36]

## How common-word brands became ownable

- **Started on a modified domain and bought the .com later:**
  - Square launched on squareup.com. [38][39]
  - Ramp used tryramp.com until its Series A. [40]
  - Notion ran on notion.so for years before buying notion.com. [41][42]
  - Zoom bought zoom.com for about $2M. [43]
  - Mercury bought mercury.com. [44]
- **Kept an alternative extension for good:** Linear (linear.app) and Arc (arc.net). [45][46]
- **Picked a word with no association in its category.** Stripe did this, then bought stripe.com through cold outreach. [47]
- **What skipping clearance costs.** Mondo was forced to rename to Monzo. [49][50]

## Assessment

- **solve: medium-high risk.**
  - **High** if the plan is to own the bare word SOLVE in software or AI classes.
  - **Medium** as a stylized consumer wordmark inside a distinctive identity.
- **Sovereign: higher risk as a consumer name.** Keep it internal, and avoid "Sovereign engine" in public.
- **SOV:** choose another ticker before any token exists.

Mitigations adopted in `BRAND.md` §Name:
- File a composite mark, not the bare word.
- Avoid "solve ai" and "Solve Intelligence".
- Use a modified domain now.
- Run no paid media before counsel clears the name.
- Build name-independent assets.
