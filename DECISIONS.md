**2026-11-09 — The visual language is the value the customer perceives; the card is its reference.** The founder: "the visual language that is the base and how it grows ... is really our value ... no matter how well it works, if the visuals aren't right, it won't work ... we gotta get the visuals of the card, the physical card, make that look real, and then it strap right down from there, like everything needs to be uniform and also customized." Recorded with the reasoning from the conversation it came from. **SOLVE owns the standard; the person owns the expression; real capabilities give that expression meaning** — three layers: the permanent frame (brand, identity, accessibility, consistent interaction, permission boundaries, authentic status, recovery), the personal expression (colour, imagery, composition, typography within readable limits, motion, atmosphere, content, AI-generated styles), and the real capability (contact, connect, publish, create, share, authorize, request payment, pay, view outcomes) — the last being what stops customisation from becoming a skin. **Three degrees of expression:** Base (official, restrained, light and dark), Personal (colour, type, imagery, layout, modules), Freeform (AI-assisted composition and custom assets inside safe boundaries); a person can always return to a dependable Base. **Protected zones, binding:** no theme may break readability, hide an essential control, alter real authority, or obscure an amount, recipient, fee, consent or settlement status — approval, consent, revocation and payment surfaces render on base tokens regardless of personal theme. **AI designs with the person, never for them,** and produces a structured, reusable configuration rather than an image someone must rebuild or code the system must rewrite; preview, edit, approve, save and undo. **The card is where the language originates, not a shape everything copies:** the wider system inherits its discipline and quality, not its rectangle; a printed card cannot change once made, while its linked digital identity changes continuously, so a new design means a replacement card. Order for the visual system: the SOV card as design reference, MySpace as expression reference, Home as private reference, then Bench and Control inherit. Payments stay in the long-term card vision but are built as a reusable capability before any physical payment card: an NFC business-card tag is not an EMV payment credential, and tap-to-pay requires an issuer programme, a certified secure element and compliance. **Engineering consequence, recorded because it decides the build:** `lib/product/appearance.ts` already implements the restricted presentation contract and the propose-then-accept flow, but appearance lives in Supabase account metadata — unversioned, unexportable, unsavable by a guest, unpublishable, and applied to some surfaces but not to the shell itself, which is why Home and the rest currently show two base skins. Appearance becomes a typed, versioned record in the person's own scope, with a published version as the single source MySpace and the card's digital destination render from; that lands before MySpace and the card, since both depend on it. The standard: "As personal as MySpace. As intuitive as an iPhone. As dependable as a serious financial instrument. As adaptable as the person using it."

**2026-11-09 — The review standard, and the compounding rule.** The founder, closing a long day of specification: "The important thing now is not to redesign the foundation again." Work is judged against four things. **Integration:** did new capabilities reuse the existing kernel rather than create parallel systems? **Human experience:** can someone immediately understand SOLVE, personalise their space, and do something useful? **Independent value:** do ID, MySpace, Bench, Space and Control each provide value without requiring the whole ecosystem? **Real functionality:** can a person save, reopen, create, share and control actual things — not just see attractive interfaces? Plus one new rule, recorded as binding: **every new feature should strengthen at least one other feature without becoming a dependency for it.** And the question that replaces "how much was built": *what can a real person now accomplish that they could not accomplish yesterday, and what has actually been verified?* Each day's answer belongs in `docs/handoff/`. Also noted from the same day's social specification, so it is not lost while not being built: MySpace belongs under ID as the person's public-facing environment while Home stays genuinely private; a person's private AI, an optional public AI representative limited to owner-published material, and a collaborator's agents are the same actor architecture with different permissions; AI authority is graded observe → suggest → prepare → act, with scope, destination, time window, delay, cap and revocation; creations have multiple linked representations rather than six copies; and the social failure modes to design against are a mandatory infinite feed, forced public popularity scores, engagement-based notification defaults and concealed AI participation. None of that changes the standing build order.

**2026-11-09 — The foundation, fixed: one shell, five destinations, eight shared concepts, five human actions.** Founder specification, stated as the architecture to protect from further expansion or redesign. The **five human actions** are *Find · Bring in · Create · Share · Act*, with inspect, control and recover available throughout — a tighter spine than the five destinations and the thing to design against. The **eight shared concepts** beneath every capability are identity (who), things (what), relationships (how connected), capabilities (what is possible), authority (who may act), actions (what happened), evidence (what supports a claim) and history (what changed); recovery, privacy and portability protect all eight. Each belongs to the kernel already. **The five-principle test, adopted as the gate before any capability is called foundational:** useful alone (a human benefits without activating the rest of SOLVE); familiar immediately (understood without learning our terminology); grounded in reality (the screen accurately represents what exists and what happened); within human control (access understood, permission withdrawn, recovery and departure possible); reusable foundation (other kinds of people use the same mechanism with no duplicate code). Any missing principle is a design question to resolve before treating the capability as foundational. **Independently valuable does not mean independently built:** a public identity works without a project, a saved photograph without AI, a community without crypto — all sharing one identity, permission, event and storage architecture. **The boundary on compounding, which is a safety rule and not a preference:** compounding comes through reusable mechanisms and deliberately shared creations, never through personal data or AI context moving between people or between private Spaces on its own. **"Make anything a starting point"** is named as the underappreciated feature and the clearest separation from an ordinary dashboard — a photograph can begin a construction project, a conversation a shared decision, a document an authorized action, without the person needing to understand the mechanism. Those possibilities are **contextual, never universal buttons shown on every object regardless of sense**, and they are the same thing as part five of the six-part record ("what's next"): one real action, or nothing. Five people opening SOLVE — a parent, a creator, a contractor, two brothers, and someone with no project at all — are each a complete and legitimate use; their own activity decides what the environment becomes, and none of them selects a vertical or a different edition. Enumerating a capability in this specification is not a commitment to build it next; the test above decides, and the standing build order is unchanged.

**2026-11-09 — Card-first design constitution: a permanent universal frame, unlimited personal expression inside it.** Founder specification. The physical SOV card sets the trust, visual discipline, identity continuity and interaction philosophy for the whole application — not because every screen should look like a card, but because a card's frame is universal, its contents personal, its function precise and its authority verifiable. **Three things are separated and must never be conflated:** the SOLVE **identity** (the person and their authoritative record), the **SOV card** (a physical instrument representing that identity and, later, narrowly defined capabilities), and the **public profile** (only what the owner explicitly chose to show). Losing the card must not mean losing the person; the card is replaceable and the identity persists. The first real card release carries exactly three functions — *identify* (a scan opens the published public identity), *connect* (approved contact or collaboration actions), *control* (the owner updates the published identity, withdraws published information, or revokes the instrument). Explicitly forbidden on it: fictional payment, government-identity, cryptographic or certification claims, payment-network marks, and decorative verification symbols presented as issued credentials. The founder states the limit himself: "A QR code alone isn't secure proof that its presenter is the owner" — cryptographic authorization and offline-verifiable credentials wait for secure hardware, verification, integration and legal review, which matches the offline-verifiable-authority requirement recorded earlier today. **What SOLVE controls:** identity structure, security, permission rules, provenance, recovery, accessibility. **What the human controls:** appearance, chosen name, personal content, arrangement, tools, creations, shared experiences. The analogy is a house — electrical standards and structural integrity stay dependable while the person chooses the architecture's expression and the whole interior; so a monochrome default never prevents a child's Space being colourful, an artist's being immersive or a developer's being highly technical. Officialness comes from consistent function, accurate representation, secure authority and reliable behaviour, never from simulated credentials. Each surface has a recognisable character: Home a personal home screen, ID a personal identity instrument, Space a personal place, Bench the appropriate real tool with the brand out of the way, Control trustworthy device settings. **Dependencies this creates, recorded because the card cannot ship without them:** the narrow anonymous public-read path (authorized in principle 2026-11-09, not designed yet) is what a scan actually resolves to; publication withdrawal and instrument revocation need to be real, not described; and the public-identity work already applied to staging (`sov_public_identity`, `public_space_projection`) has **no migration file in this repository**, so it is not currently reproducible and must be reconciled before anything depends on it. Promise: "Your digital life. In your hands." — meaningful only when the environment delivers the same reliability as the object representing it.

**2026-11-09 — Identity recovery is scoped, declared in advance, and never climbs.** The founder, asked how a person who loses a key, phone or email becomes themselves again without creating a party who could impersonate them: "Depends on the scope." Recovery requirements are a property of the scope and tighten as what is at stake grows — a casual personal identity, one carrying confirmed work, one carrying money or regulated activity. Two conditions make that safe rather than merely flexible. Recovering a scope grants nothing above it, so a compromised low-stakes identity cannot climb into a high-stakes one; `kernel.scope_covers` already expresses the containment. And the method is declared before it is needed and cannot be loosened by whoever is recovering, because a person choosing their recovery during a crisis is a person being social-engineered. Because there is no onboarding wizard, the product asks for a recovery method at the moment stakes first rise — the first confirmed work, the first money, the first grant to another person — which is also the moment the person understands why it matters. Identity recovery is added to the load-bearing mechanism list; it was absent from it before today.

**2026-11-09 — Reconciliation with the external design review: seven corrections accepted, the build order unchanged.** A first-principles review (via ChatGPT) was reconciled against the day's decisions. Accepted as corrections: (1) authority is not automatically safe — a signing key can authorize spending even when it stores no value, so safety comes from limits on what the key may authorize and from separating recovery from spending authority, not from the category choice; (2) a signed obligation is not a payment — it is a verifiable promise, and settlement still needs a live rail and confirmation; (3) the earlier claim that an authority-only card avoids licensing was too broad, since FinCEN's line turns on whether value is intermediated, not on vocabulary; (4) a universal record does not require a universal visual layout — the six parts are the data contract and must always be inspectable, while the generic view is the guaranteed view and the inspector, not the only presentation: the same authoritative thing may also appear as a calendar, a map, an editor or a canvas; (5) a person is more than their unfinished work — unresolved has priority in ordering on the surface that asks what needs you, not a monopoly on presence, and Space and ID hold what was made and who the person is; (6) identity recovery was a genuine omission (recorded separately above); (7) the three-layer separation — truth, capability, expression — is adopted as the naming of what the repo already separates, and the review's acceptance test replaces the earlier visual target: can a person enter, recognize what is theirs, do something meaningful, understand the consequence, and remain in control, with no explanation? Clarified, not conceded: independent usefulness never meant independent infrastructure (one kernel, shared), and claim/evidence/confirmation/dispute were already separated earlier today. Held: the build order does not widen. The earlier phrasing "nothing else is load-bearing" overstated what was a list of what is load-bearing next; but the verified repository state — 39 open pull requests, six separate Space implementations, four project clones, zero storage buckets — shows the demonstrated failure mode here is building capability before its mechanisms exist, so creation tools and environment composition are real and are not next. Amended list: chain anchor, counterparty confirmation, contest, narrow public read, identity recovery, plus the authority dial and the guaranteed thing view as surfaces, with evidence storage as a hard infrastructure dependency. The next visual, superseding the earlier four-surface brief, is one ordinary person taking one real thing from beginning to verified consequence.

**2026-11-09 — Crypto is optional, and a necessary option; no one feature is bigger than the person.** The founder: "the crypto is of ocurse optinal but neccesary option. no one feature is biggetr than person." The chain is never load-bearing for anything except ownership of chain assets, which is the line `docs/MONETARY-CONSTITUTION.md` Part I already draws: identity, record, authority and obligations live in the kernel; ownership and supply live on the chain. The consequence that makes the option safe — lose your key and you lose your crypto, not yourself. Any design in which key loss destroys identity, history or proof of work has made a feature bigger than the person and does not ship. An obligation is denominated in whatever unit the person actually uses and the rail is chosen at settlement, so a job invoiced in dollars and a settlement in SOLVE are one record with a different edge. No surface says wallet or connect; a chain rail appears only as the origin on a provenance line, exactly as any other origin does, and a person who never touches it sees no holes, nothing greyed out and no disabled state — the same treatment optional AI gets. The test applied to every mechanism from here: can the person leave, revoke it, and keep everything? Export answers that for the record, self-custody for value, revocation for authority. A mechanism that fails it does not ship however much leverage it offers.

**2026-11-09 — The SOV card must work when banks do not, which makes its primitive authority rather than value.** The founder, after a conversation with ChatGPT: "the sov card literally has to have live crypto payments so if banks fail it still works. that extrpoalates to build." Recorded with the reasoning, because the requirement is already canon and the naive reading of it is wrong. Already canon: Monetary Constitution Part II law 6, "Users may leave, self-custody, send, trade, and use external rails. Fiat providers are replaceable edges." The correction: a fiat-backed stablecoin's reserves sit in banks, so a bank failure breaks the peg first — resilience therefore rests on SOLVE being a real reserve, not on crypto generically. And the harder problem than settlement is acceptance: what works when banking stops is verifiable mutual obligation, which the kernel already does better than any chain. So the card carries a claim and settlement routes over whatever rail is alive; it is not a wallet. The minimum system, in dependency order: identity not dependent on an issuer (exists, less key custody); authority that verifies offline (does not exist anywhere — the card's real engineering problem, and it requires a chip that can sign, not a tag holding a URL, which must be known before hardware is ordered); obligation as a record (partly exists); exactly-once settlement (specified in Part VI, partly implemented); rail routing with replaceable edges (exists); verification without trusting the operator (missing — the chain anchor); acceptance (missing — counterparty confirmation). The last two are already the top of the build order, so the end state validated the roadmap rather than changing it. Money transmission licensing (FinCEN, and Florida MSB) is a parallel non-engineering track that must start earlier than the code, because it is slower. Open and not yet answered by the founder: whether the card holds value or only authority.

**2026-11-09 — Integrate first.** The founder: "also a core principle is intergrate first." Two meanings, both binding. In the product: SOLVE never requires a person to leave the tools they already use or re-enter what already exists. Things arrive from photographs, messages, emails, documents and spoken description, and the record states where each came from. No import wizard, no migration step; integration happens per thing, at the moment it is useful. The provenance element must therefore be able to name an origin outside the system — from a photo, from a text, from an email, entered by hand — with the same treatment as an act performed inside it; an externally originated thing is a first-class record, not a lesser one. In the build: nothing counts until it is landed and working together. Generation is cheap and integration is scarce, so one mechanism is finished and on the trunk before the next starts. The repo's 39 open branches, 6 duplicate Space implementations and 4 project clones are the evidence of what happens without this rule.

**2026-11-09 — Every mechanism is independently complete; no mechanism has a prerequisite.** The founder: "they can enter in from any point each mechnism has to be indviually max value", and "we skip no corners on value or integrity of each mechnsism everything is intentional." There is no funnel and no onboarding chain — each surface is a front door. The test applied to every mechanism: what is this worth to someone who has nothing else? A public identity must be worth something to a person who records nothing; one proven thing must be worth something with no type defined, no AI and no collaborator; an export must be worth something to someone leaving. If the answer requires another part, the mechanism is not finished. The consequence accepted: fewer mechanisms ship, each complete, because a mechanism at 80% is negative value for a system whose claim is that the record is true.

**2026-11-09 — A stranger may read what is deliberately public, and learn nothing else.** The founder, asked whether a mechanism must be valuable to someone who never signs in: "yes and we skip no corners on value or integrity of each mechnsism everything is intentional." An anonymous read path is authorized in principle. Its integrity conditions, set before design: it serves only what an owner explicitly marked public; there is no listing and no enumerating; and it must be impossible to learn anything about what was not made public — including through differing responses for "private" and "does not exist", since that difference is itself a leak. To be delivered as a migration plus tests that actively attempt the leaks, for the founder's approval before anything is applied. The kernel schema stays closed to API roles; nothing is widened.

**2026-11-09 — Claim, proof and confirmation are never the same field, for anyone.** The founder, asked who may treat an interpretation as fact: "what reflect truth and relaity here." Reality distinguishes three things and the design keeps them distinct: the artifact is fact (a photograph exists, a message arrived at a time); the interpretation is a claim and has an author; the confirmation is an act, with its own actor, authority and time. An AI may propose an interpretation of incoming material — the highest-value use of AI in the product — and the proposal is visibly a claim until someone confirms it. The rule has no exception for humans: a person's own typed entry is their word, not proof, and no actor may author its own proof. This is why the AI's full reach into reality is safe without a veto: an actor operating at scale still cannot manufacture one verified fact.

**2026-11-09 — AI is an extension of the human that can affect reality, with a stop the human sets.** The founder: "it should be able to do nothing or literally ebverything that is counsive to vision of the human", and "we want the ai to be an extention of human that can affect reality." Authority runs as one dial from nothing to everything, granted per goal rather than globally, so an actor cannot be helpful in a direction the human never pointed at, and is revocable from the surface where the work is. The dial reaches outbound acts that cannot be recalled. What makes that safe is time and record, not a veto: intent is recorded before the act and the result after; the human sets a delay per scope (none for a crew message, minutes for a customer update, never for a regulated submission) and the stop lives on the surface where the thing is pending. Irreversible acts carry visible weight, and that weight is information.

**2026-11-09 — Truth outward: attribution is stated, once, plainly.** The founder, asked whether a recipient is told an act was carried out by an AI: "whatever is based in truth." The recipient is told. Not a disclaimer and not an apology — accurate attribution, the way a letter says who wrote it and on whose behalf. The corollary binds the record itself: it may never say something happened that did not. No "sent" where it failed, no "verified" where someone merely said so, no "complete" where the evidence is absent. Absence is shown as plainly as presence. Florida insurance is a regulated channel and may impose stricter disclosure than the trade scopes, which is one reason the dial is per scope; the law is to be checked before that scope ships.

**2026-11-09 — Fixed frame, personal contents — the card is literal, not a metaphor.** The founder: "when you connect the idea of the solve phsycial card being litteral you can extrpoalte back in language often as well." Every bank card on earth has identical dimensions, chip placement and field positions, and that rigidity is exactly what lets it work in any machine anywhere; its contents are entirely personal, and nobody experiences it as a constraint. SOLVE obeys the same rule, and the SOV card obeys it physically. The frame is the six irreducible parts every thing has, always present, always in this order, never rearranged or hidden: what it is; whose scope it lives in; what has happened to it, in order, with who acted and under what authority; whether it is proven, with evidence shown or its absence shown just as plainly; the one next action; who else can reach it, revocable in place. A record whose parts are optional proves nothing. The surface names are literal descriptions, not branding: ID is identity, Space is where your things are, Bench is where you work on one thing, Control is the dial on authority, and solve means resolving something — which is why they need no onboarding and survive into any domain.
